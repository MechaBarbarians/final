To play Craps, press the Play Craps! button which will roll your two dice. If the result is = to 7 or 11, CRAPS You lose. If you get doubles of an even number you win! Reroll if you push.

To Play doubles, press the button to roll two dice, if both numbers are the same you win!

If you want to check if a word is a palindrome, type it in the box and press the button!
